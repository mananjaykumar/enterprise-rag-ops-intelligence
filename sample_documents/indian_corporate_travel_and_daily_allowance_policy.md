# Bharat Enterprise Solutions Corporate Travel & Daily Expense Policy 2026

## 1. Scope & Objective
This policy governs all official business travel, lodging, and daily meal allowances for employees across India. All business travel must be pre-approved by the respective Department Head via the corporate HRMS portal.

## 2. City Classification for Domestic Travel
To align with cost-of-living variations across India, cities are classified into two standard tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Metro Cities):** Mumbai, New Delhi & NCR (Gurugram, Noida), Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata.
- **Tier 2 (Non-Metro Cities):** Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Kochi, Lucknow, and all other state capitals and regional hubs.

## 3. Daily Meal & Incidental Spending Limits
The daily spending limit for official meals is capped based on city tier:
- **Tier 1 (Metro) Daily Meal Limit:** Strictly **₹1,500.00 per day**
  - Breakfast allowance: ₹300.00
  - Lunch allowance: ₹500.00
  - Dinner allowance: ₹700.00
- **Tier 2 (Non-Metro) Daily Meal Limit:** Strictly **₹1,000.00 per day**
  - Breakfast allowance: ₹200.00
  - Lunch allowance: ₹350.00
  - Dinner allowance: ₹450.00
Any meal expense exceeding the daily ceiling requires written prior authorization from the Functional Director. Alcoholic beverages, tips exceeding 10%, and personal mini-bar charges are strictly non-reimbursable.

## 4. Hotel Accommodation & Lodging Limits
- **Tier 1 (Metro):** Standard hotel room up to **₹4,500.00 per night** (exclusive of applicable GST).
- **Tier 2 (Non-Metro):** Standard hotel room up to **₹3,000.00 per night** (exclusive of applicable GST).
Booking luxury or 5-star executive suites requires written sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## 5. Local Conveyance & Cab Reimbursals
- Standard cab services (Ola, Uber, prepaid airport taxis) are reimbursable up to **₹800.00 per one-way trip** upon submitting the automated digital GST invoice.
- Use of personal four-wheeler vehicle for official intra-city travel is reimbursed at the approved rate of **₹14.00 per kilometer**.
