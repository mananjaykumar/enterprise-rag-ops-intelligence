# Bharat Enterprise Solutions Vendor Procurement & GST Compliance Policy 2026

## 1. Governance & Onboarding Requirements
Every external vendor, contractor, or software vendor providing services to Bharat Enterprise Solutions within India must complete statutory onboarding before contracts are awarded.
Mandatory statutory documents include:
- Valid 15-digit Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN).
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) card copy.
- MSME / Udyam Registration Certificate (if registered under the MSMED Act, 2006).
- Bank account cancelled cheque with valid IFSC code.

## 2. Procurement Authorization Thresholds
Departmental purchase orders and vendor contracts must be approved according to the following authorization limits:
- **Contracts up to ₹5,00,000.00:** Department Head / General Manager approval.
- **Contracts between ₹5,00,000.00 and ₹25,00,000.00:** Vice President and Procurement Committee approval.
- **Contracts exceeding ₹25,00,000.00:** Managing Director (MD) and Chief Financial Officer (CFO) written approval.
- **Single-Source Exceptions:** Requires written clearance from the Internal Audit Committee.

## 3. Statutory TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) Deductions
The Finance & Accounts department will deduct withholding tax (TDS) as per the Indian Income Tax Act 1961:
- **Section 194C (Contracts & Subcontractors):** 1.0% for Individual/HUF vendors; 2.0% for Corporate vendors.
- **Section 194J (Professional & Technical Services):** 2.0% for technical services / call centers; 10.0% for professional advisory services.
- **Section 194Q (Purchase of Goods exceeding ₹50 Lakhs):** 0.1% on purchase value exceeding ₹50 Lakhs in a financial year.

## 4. Payment Cycles & MSME Compliance
- **Registered MSME Suppliers:** Payment must be released strictly within **45 days** from invoice acceptance date, as mandated by Section 15 of the MSMED Act 2006.
- **Standard Corporate Vendors:** Payment term is **Net-30 days** following formal three-way matching (PO, Goods/Service Receipt Note, and e-Invoice).
- Invoices must feature valid IRN (Invoice Reference Number) and QR code if the vendor is subject to mandatory GST e-Invoicing.
