# Cyberdyne Systems Vendor Procurement & Contract Management Policy 2026

## 1. Scope & Objective
This policy defines the governance, approval thresholds, and risk management criteria for onboarding external vendors, contractors, and software suppliers across all Cyberdyne operational units.

## 2. Procurement Approval Thresholds
All departmental expenditures involving third-party vendors must comply with the following authorization matrix:
- **Contracts under $25,000.00:** Department Manager approval required.
- **Contracts between $25,000.00 and $100,000.00:** Department Head and Procurement Director approval required.
- **Contracts exceeding $100,000.00:** Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer written sign-off required.
- **Sole-Source Contracts:** Any single-source vendor contract without competitive bidding must include an Executive Justification Form.

## 3. Vendor Risk Assessment & Security Review
Before executing any master service agreement (MSA), the Information Security Team must complete a Vendor Risk Review:
- **Low-Risk Vendors:** Standard non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and vendor intake form.
- **Medium-Risk Vendors:** Annual SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 audit report verification.
- **High-Risk Vendors (accessing proprietary code or employee PII):** Comprehensive penetration test results, dedicated tenant data isolation audit, and VP of Cybersecurity approval.

## 4. Invoicing and Payment Terms
- The standard payment term across all operational vendors is strictly **Net-30** from invoice receipt date.
- Invoices submitted without a matching Purchase Order (PO) number will be automatically rejected.
- Early payment discounts (e.g., 2% Net-10) should be prioritized by the Accounts Payable department whenever cash flow permits.
