# Cyberdyne Systems Employee Wellness & Continuing Education Policy 2026

## 1. Purpose and Eligibility
Cyberdyne Systems provides comprehensive professional development and health stipends to support employee growth, work-life balance, and continuous skill advancement. All full-time employees are eligible upon completing 90 days of continuous employment.

## 2. Annual Wellness & Fitness Allowance
Each full-time employee is allocated an annual wellness spending allowance of strictly **$600.00 per calendar year**.
- **Eligible Expenses:** Gym memberships, fitness equipment, yoga studio passes, mental health and meditation app subscriptions, and ergonomic standing desks.
- **Ineligible Expenses:** Supplements, personal apparel, spa treatments, or sports event tickets.
- **Reimbursement Deadline:** All wellness claims must be submitted via the expense portal within 60 days of purchase with itemized receipts.

## 3. Professional Education & Conference Stipend
Full-time technical staff and engineers are eligible for an annual professional development allowance of up to **$2,500.00 per year**:
- **Eligible Programs:** Accredited AI/ML courses, cloud certification exams (AWS, GCP, Azure), security credentials (CISSP, CISA), and technical conference attendance (registration fees and conference travel).
- **Approval Workflow:** Formal manager approval is required at least 3 weeks prior to course registration or conference attendance.
- **Post-Certification Verification:** Employees must upload a completion certificate to HR within 30 days of passing the examination.
